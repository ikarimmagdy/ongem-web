OnGem cards are issued by a licensed UAE bank. You manage them in OnGem on the web and on your phone.

## Freeze and unfreeze

If a card looks wrong, freeze it. Payments on a frozen card are declined until you unfreeze it. Both take one tap and your second sign-in step.

## Card details OnGem never sees

When you show the card number, the PIN or set a PIN, the bank's own secure window opens inside OnGem. The number goes from the bank to your screen; it never passes through OnGem's servers. The window closes by itself after **5 minutes**.

## Something you do not recognise?

Open the card and choose **Dispute a charge**. It starts a support request about that card, and you follow the replies and the status in one thread.
