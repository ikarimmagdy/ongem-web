Most payment mistakes are caught by a second look. That is why OnGem sends larger payments, and money you pull in from another bank, to a second person before anything moves.

## How approvals work

1. Someone in your company creates the payment and confirms it is them with a second sign-in step.
2. The payment waits, marked **Pending approval**. Nothing has moved yet.
3. A different person in your company who is allowed to approve payments reviews it and approves or declines it, again with their own second step.
4. Only then does the payment go to the bank.

The person who created a payment can never approve it. The rules are kept per company.

## What if you are the only person?

Many companies start with one founder. In that case there is no second person to ask, so **OnGem approves it**. You will see:

> Waiting for OnGem to approve it, because no one else in your company can approve payments. Nothing has moved yet.

The approve and decline buttons are not shown to you, because you made the payment. When you add someone to your company who can approve, approvals go back to your own team.
