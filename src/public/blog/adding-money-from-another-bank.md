You can fund your OnGem account by wiring money in, or by pulling it from your company's account at another bank. This post covers the second.

## Step 1: link the account

Add the other bank's routing and account number and who owns the account. The bank checks that the account is real and belongs to your company. Until it is verified, it shows as unverified and cannot be pulled from.

_Coming soon: linking an account instantly by signing in to your other bank. Today accounts are typed in and verified by the bank._

## Step 2: add money

1. Choose the verified account and the OnGem account to fund.
2. Enter the amount and review it.
3. Confirm it is you with your second sign-in step.
4. A second person in your company approves it, or OnGem does if no one else can approve.
5. The bank pulls the money by ACH.

## Limits and timing

Today a single top-up can be up to **US$25,000**, and a company can add up to **US$50,000** a day. ACH takes one to two business days, and the bank can hold money pulled in this way for a short time before you can spend it.
