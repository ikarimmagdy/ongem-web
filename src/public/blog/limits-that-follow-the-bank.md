Like every business bank account, an OnGem account has limits on how much can move: per payment, per day and per month.

## Where the limits come from

Piermont Bank, the bank that holds your account, reviews every business it banks and keeps that review up to date. OnGem reads the bank's view of your business every day, and again straight away when the bank tells us it has changed. The limits that apply to your payments follow it.

That means:

- **A new company may start with lower limits.** As your company builds a history with the bank, limits can rise.
- **Limits can change.** When they do, OnGem sends you a notice that your limits have changed.
- **Some features need a history.** For example, pulling money in from another bank can need a period of payments without returns first.

## What you see when a payment is over a limit

OnGem tells you plainly that the payment is over the limit, for one payment or for the day or month, and that nothing was sent. It never moves part of a payment.

## The limits we use today

Until the bank confirms the limits for our pilot, OnGem uses its built-in limits: **US$25,000 per payment and US$50,000 per day**. The limits per level that we proposed to the bank are waiting for its approval.
