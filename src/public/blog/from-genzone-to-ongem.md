GenZone forms companies and handles business licences. When you form your company with GenZone, you can ask it to send your company's details to OnGem.

## What happens

1. GenZone sends your company's details through OnGem's Partner API, with your consent.
2. You sign in to OnGem and find your application already filled in: the company, its address and its owners.
3. Each owner adds their own identity details in OnGem.
4. You confirm the details, accept the bank's disclosures and submit.
5. Piermont Bank verifies the business and its owners, and OnGem shows you each step.

No retyping what you already gave GenZone, and nothing is submitted to the bank until you do it yourself.
