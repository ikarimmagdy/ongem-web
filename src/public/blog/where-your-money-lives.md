OnGem is a financial technology company, not a bank. When you open a business account with OnGem, the account is held at **Piermont Bank**, through **Synctera**, the banking platform that connects OnGem to the bank.

## What that means for you

- **The bank holds the deposits.** The balance you see in OnGem is the bank's balance, read from the bank. OnGem shows it; it does not keep your money in its own accounts.
- **The bank decides who can open an account.** When you apply, Piermont Bank verifies your business and its owners. OnGem shows you each step and asks for anything the bank needs, such as an ID document.
- **Every night we check our view against the bank's.** If the balance or a transaction we show ever differs from the bank's records, our nightly check flags it so it is corrected.

## What OnGem adds on top

The bank account is the foundation. OnGem gives you the tools to run it: payments by ACH, scheduled payments, virtual cards, approvals for larger payments, notices when something needs you, and support that answers in the same place.

## Records

OnGem keeps customer records for seven years after an account closes, and tells you so when you open and when you close an account.
